# Planning Steps

The development of this Captsone project has been separated into two steps, one of which is dedicated to Planning. This step consists of the Concept Analysis Requirements Design portion of CARDBTP

## Concept

The first part of a plan is developing an idea. I represented this stage by maaking a list of candidate ideas and choosing the final one based on feasability and how well it incorporated the concepts that I learned.

## Analysis

Analysis is breaking up a goal into small manageable chuncks such as the materials necessary as well as looking at alternative pathways to reach a similar result. 

## Requirements 

This step is unique to the others as it can and should run in parallel to the rest of the planning phase. Thinking about requirements is a requisite for any successful project so naturally I included it in this phase. 

## Design

The final part of planning iss coming up with an effective design. For this I included a detailed cirucit diagram and a more general build layout.
